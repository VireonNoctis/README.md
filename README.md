## 01 — A little about me
I enjoy building useful software, understanding how systems work, and polishing details that make projects feel intentional. Most of my time goes into bots, community tooling, web projects, automation, and experiments that become too interesting to leave alone.

Outside code: anime, music, rabbit holes, and an unreasonable number of open tabs.

> **Main line:** “Things I can’t leave alone.” Build with curiosity, improve with intent, and keep the details honest.
